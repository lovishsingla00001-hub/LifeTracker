How it's calculated

A weighted average: habits use today's check-ins; goals use progress toward this month's targets; focus uses completed LifeTrack minutes against 30 minutes; wellness averages available steps, water, sleep, and mood against their targets. A daily task counts only when completed. Missing categories are omitted, not scored as zero. Exercise time is not scored, so skipping a workout on a rest day does not lower the score.

Adjust category weights

These are relative priorities. The score rebalances over categories that have data. Weights stay on this device for this account.
