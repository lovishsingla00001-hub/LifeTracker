How it's calculated

A weighted average: habits use today's check-ins; goals use progress toward this month's targets; focus uses completed LifeTrack minutes against 30 minutes; wellness averages available steps, water, sleep, and mood against their targets. A daily task counts only when completed. Missing categories are omitted, not scored as zero. Exercise time is not scored, so skipping a workout on a rest day does not lower the score.

Adjust category weights

These are relative priorities. The score rebalances over categories that have data. Weights stay on this device for this account.

Build a friendly challenge

Find friends who opted in, see online/offline status, and invite accepted friends into a 90-day group match. Both accounts need to be signed in on the same hosted Worker, and each person must enable sharing. Localhost databases are separate on each computer. The match starts when you create it; friends who accept later are scored from the day they join.

Only your daily Life Score, habit completion %, focus minutes, streak days, and steps are shared with friends who accept the same match. Habit names, body details, and raw tracker history are not shared. Turning sharing off removes your saved match metrics.
