# LifeTrack — Habit & Wellness

**Build your life. One habit at a time.**

LifeTrack is a habit and wellness tracking application designed to help people build consistent routines, manage personal goals, and keep track of everyday wellness activities in one place.

## 🔗 Project Links

- **Project Live Link:** https://lovishsingla00001-hub.github.io/LifeTracker/
- **Microsoft Store:** Search for **LifeTrack: Habit & Wellness** in Microsoft Store.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Habit Tracking** — Create and track daily habits.
- **Goal Management** — Set personal goals and monitor progress.
- **Focus Sessions** — Support focused work and study sessions.
- **Water Tracking** — Keep a record of daily water intake.
- **Sleep Tracking** — Record sleep information and review routines.
- **Fitness Tracking** — Track steps and exercise-related activity.
- **Progress & Streaks** — Review consistency and personal progress.
- **Google Sign-In and Guest Access** — Choose how to use the app.
- **Google Drive Backup** — Back up supported app data to your Google account.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many people find it difficult to maintain healthy routines and stay consistent with personal goals. Habit records, fitness activity, water intake, sleep, and productivity are often tracked in separate places, making daily progress harder to manage.

LifeTrack brings these everyday tracking activities together in one application. It helps users organize routines, monitor progress, and build better habits over time.

## 💡 Our Solution

LifeTrack offers a centralized space for habit tracking, goal management, focus sessions, and selected wellness records. Its aim is to make personal progress easier to record, understand, and maintain.
